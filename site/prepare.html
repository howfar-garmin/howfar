Race prep

Get HowFar ready for your next race

HowFar learns your pace on every slope while you run. What it can't learn on its own is the course ahead. Give it the course profile, with every climb and descent between checkpoints, and it knows what's coming before you get there. Only with the profile can it also show the checkpoint after the next, and points you add. Make it here, then paste it into the Garmin Connect app. It's optional: without it, HowFar still counts down to your watch's checkpoints.

  1. Your checkpoints. Add the course file of the race you'll run, and any points you want to add.
  2. Paste on the watch. Copy the text into HowFar's settings in Garmin Connect.

Everything happens in this browser. Your files are not uploaded anywhere, and what you save stays on this device. The only exception is the optional “Add huts and villages” button, which asks OpenStreetMap what's around your course.

Step 1

Your checkpoints

The race's course file, the same one you load on the watch. HowFar finds its checkpoints and notes the slope of every stretch between them, so it knows every climb and descent ahead (the P on the watch).

Your watch tells HowFar only about the checkpoints in the course you load. Here you can add more: the top and bottom of every big climb, huts and villages along the way. HowFar counts down to them too, without changing the course on your watch. Your watch's own alerts and checkpoint list stay as they are: only HowFar knows the points you add.

Add more points

HowFar shows these too: climbs and huts found for you, or any point of your own, like a spring or a viewpoint. They start as minor points, shown on the way; make one main to count down to it. They appear in the list above, where you can switch or untick any of them.

From OpenStreetMap: named mountain huts, shelters and villages within 200 m of the course. This is the one thing on this page that goes online: it asks OpenStreetMap's search service for what's inside a rectangle around your course. Only the rectangle's corners are sent, never your file.
Or click the course chart above to pick the place.
Want your watch itself to know these points too?

HowFar doesn't need it, but if you also want your watch's own alerts, checkpoint list and ClimbPro to show the added points, download the course with every ticked checkpoint and load it on the watch instead of the original: in Garmin Connect on a computer, Training & Planning → Courses → Import.

Detail level

HowFar stores one slope value every 25 to 400 m, the finest that fits in the setting. Garmin Connect's limit on text length isn't documented; 4,000 characters works. Lower it if the app refuses the paste.

The text for the setting “Course profile”

              

              

                
                
              

            

          

        


        

          

            Where do I get the course file?
            
    
              
  1. From the race website: most races publish the official GPX, often per stage.
    2. 
              
  2. From Garmin Connect: open the course, then Export as GPX or FIT.
    3. 
              
  3. Even better: once the course is in Garmin Connect, export it again as FIT. That file has exactly the checkpoints your watch will show, so nothing has to be guessed.
    4. 
              
  4. Best is the same file you load on the watch. If the two list different checkpoints, or one is a slightly different version of the course, HowFar still works out where each checkpoint is on the profile.
    5. 
            

          

          

            A race in stages?
            
Make one profile per stage and save them all. At each life base, paste the next stage's text and load the next course on the watch. Race files often carry every checkpoint of the whole race; HowFar keeps only the ones on this stage.

          

        

      

    

  


  

    

      

        
Step 2

        
Paste the text on the watch

        
HowFar's settings are in the Garmin Connect app on your phone, with HowFar installed from the Connect IQ Store. Made the text on a computer? See below for the easiest ways to get it there.

      

      
    
        
  1. Open the Garmin Connect app and go to your watch.
    2. 
        
  2. Open Connect IQ apps (or Activities & apps), then HowFar, then Settings.
    3. 
        
  3. Paste the text into “Course profile”. Tap Save.
    4. 
        
  4. Sync the watch. On the next run with that course, the watch shows P next to the checkpoint.
    5. 
      

      

        
Made it on a computer?

        
Your library stays in the browser where you made it, so the text has to travel to where the settings are. Easiest first:

        
    
          
  1. Garmin Express, no phone needed. Connect the watch to the computer with its cable and open Garmin Express. Choose your watch, then the HowFar app and its Settings, and paste the text there.
    2. 
          
  2. Mac and iPhone. With the same Apple ID on both and Handoff on, press Copy on this page and paste straight into the Garmin Connect app on the iPhone.
    3. 
          
  3. Any phone. Paste the text into a note, an email or a message to yourself, then copy it from there on the phone.
    4. 
          
  4. Your whole library. Press Save a backup file below, open this page on the phone, Load a backup file, and copy from there.
    5.
